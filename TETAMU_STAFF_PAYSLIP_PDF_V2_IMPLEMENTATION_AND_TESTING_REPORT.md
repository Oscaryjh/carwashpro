## 5. A4 LAYOUT

## 6. HEADER

## 7. PAY SUMMARY

Gross Pay, Total Deductions and Net Pay appear in one high-level summary; Net Pay receives the strongest visual emphasis.

## 8. EMPLOYEE INFO

Employee name, code and pay basis are shown from canonical snapshots. Internal database identifiers are not shown.

## 9. ATTENDANCE

Canonical frozen attendance totals are shown only when present in the document input. The renderer does not infer attendance or payroll facts.

## 10. EARNINGS

Canonical earning components are itemized once. The section total is labelled `TOTAL GROSS EARNINGS`; a duplicate Basic/Gross row is avoided.

## 11. EMPLOYEE DEDUCTIONS

Employee-funded lines are grouped separately and use employee-facing Malaysian labels: `EPF (Employee)`, `SOCSO (Employee)`, `EIS (Employee)`, PCB and applicable CP38/LINDUNG24 lines.

## 12. EMPLOYER CONTRIBUTIONS

Employer-funded EPF/SOCSO/EIS are separate from deductions and explicitly state that they do not reduce Net Pay.

## 13. REIMBURSEMENT

Canonical claim reimbursement snapshots are shown in a separate non-wage section and do not inflate Gross Pay.

## 14. NET PAY

Net Pay is the strongest final financial band and follows the canonical frozen `netPay` value; no client-side recalculation is introduced.

## 15. NOTES

Notes are sanitized for employee presentation. Technical fixture/debug namespaces do not dominate the page footer.

## 16. ZERO VALUE POLICY
